# Help, Issues, and Requests

Choose the channel that matches what you need:

- [Get Help on Discord](https://discord.gg/duneawakeningdocker) for setup questions, troubleshooting, logs, and community assistance.
- [Report an Issue](https://github.com/Red-Blink/dune-awakening-selfhost-docker/issues) when you can provide a reproducible project bug.
- [Browse the Main Repository](https://github.com/Red-Blink/dune-awakening-selfhost-docker) for releases, source, and contribution history.
- [Explore DuneDocker.app](https://dunedocker.app/) for the project website and public server directory.

## A Useful Report Includes

- Console and game-server version.
- What you expected and what happened.
- The exact page, action, map, or player involved.
- Relevant screenshots and the smallest useful section of logs.
- Whether the behavior also occurs without a Community Addon.

Remove passwords, tokens, private IP details, Steam identifiers, and other secrets before sharing logs publicly.
