# Day-to-Day Operations

## Daily

- Check Home readiness and unexpected service restarts.
- Review online players and recent administrative actions.
- Confirm scheduled backups are succeeding.
- Investigate repeated map cycling, memory pressure, or storage growth before it becomes an outage.

## Weekly

- Review available game and Console updates.
- Download at least one known-good backup off the host.
- Check addon updates and new permission requests.
- Review public listing and Player Portal privacy choices.
- Confirm Coriolis/restart behavior and scheduled messages still align with the intended timezone.

## Before Risky Changes

Take a backup, confirm which maps/players are affected, notify players, and verify the final state. Guided Console actions create safety backups where appropriate, but that does not replace a regular off-host backup plan.
