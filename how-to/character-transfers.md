# Allow or Block Character Transfers

Open **Admin Tools → Character Transfer Settings**. These rules apply to the Battlegroup, not to one player's Console account.

## Incoming and Outgoing Rules

Choose the incoming policy: accept all, block incoming transfers, accept from private servers, or accept from official servers. Separately choose whether outgoing transfers are accepted.

The **Free Transfers** options describe transfers to or from this server. They do not override a blocked direction. Leave advanced timeout and source-deletion options unchanged unless you understand their effect.

Save the settings. Applying them restarts the Director so it reloads the rules; schedule the change when a brief interruption to login or travel coordination is acceptable.

These are the server's acceptance rules, not a guarantee that Funcom permits a particular character, account, or source-to-destination route. If a transfer fails, record both servers, the transfer direction, the exact error and time; do not delete the original character as a troubleshooting step.

## What Visitors See on dunedocker.app

Servers that report their rules can show incoming and outgoing transfer information on their public listing:

- **In: All**, **Private Only**, **Official Only**, or **Blocked**.
- **Out: Allowed** or **Blocked**.
- **(Free)** only for a direction that permits transfers and reports that it is free.
- **Not Reported** when the website has not received the rules. This does not mean transfers are blocked.

An older Console may not report these fields. Update it and allow the directory to refresh. Visitors do not need to create a character or enable the Player Portal just to read reported public transfer rules.
