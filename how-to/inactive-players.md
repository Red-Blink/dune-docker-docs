# Hide Inactive Players

Use **Hide Inactive Players After** on the Players page to keep old, offline entries out of your normal view.

The choices are **Never**, **1 Week**, **2 Weeks**, **3 Weeks**, **1 Month**, and **2 Months**. The initial default is **Never**. Changing the dropdown saves the preference and refreshes the list automatically; there is no separate Save button.

This is a display filter, not character cleanup. It does not delete characters, bases, vehicles, or inventories. Choose **Never** when you need to find an older player. Online players are not hidden just because their previous activity was old.

Character deletion and recovery are separate administrative actions. See [Players and Guilds](../console/players-and-guilds.md).
